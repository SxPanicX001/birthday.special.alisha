How to use this

  • Moving aroundSwipe left or right, or press Back and Next. The dots jump to any screen. Next glows when a screen is finished.
  • Pip, your guideThe little glass friend at the top. Tap Pip any time for a hint about the screen you're on.
  • Looking aroundTilt your phone, or drag your finger across the screen, and the glass shifts in 3D.
  • 1. The candleTap the candle or the button to blow it out. Tap again to relight it.
  • 2. The cardsTap each card to flip it. There are six.
  • 3. The wishesTap the floating bubbles before they drift away. Each one catches a wish.
  • 4. The gardenTap anywhere inside the glass to plant a flower. Plant eight.
  • 5. Open whenTap each note to open it. Scroll if the notes run past the screen.
  • 6. The giftTap the gift box to open it.
  • 7. Scratch cardRub the silver area with your finger until the surprise shows. A Reveal button appears if you get stuck.
  • 8. Memory gameTap two cards at a time and find all six matching pairs.
  • 9. The cakeTap Candle, Cherry and Sprinkles to decorate it. Add six things.
  • 10. The quizAnswer three questions to see which one you are.
  • 11. The letterTap the envelope. The letter writes itself. Tap it to skip ahead, and scroll to read.
  • 12. The finaleTap anywhere for fireworks. Press Start over to see it all again.
  • SoundThe note button at the top right turns the soft chimes on or off.
